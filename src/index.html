Our Story

It Started With Three People and a Calling

Bishop Dennis Cancel has been saved for more than twenty years. God placed a calling on his life to go and speak the good news of Jesus Christ, and to teach the truth straight from Scripture.

New Beginnings in Christ began with just three people: Bishop Cancel, his wife, and his cousin. They didn't have a building or a budget, but they had faith and the Word of God. Today our doors are open to anyone looking for the right journey to heaven.
